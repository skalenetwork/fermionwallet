Pay the vendor with owner signatures alone

Propose a 100 dUSD payout to the vendor. Safe{Wallet} opens its normal review. Sign and execute it: the owner signature is valid, and the Guard still refuses it because no quantum pre-approval exists. This is what an attacker with a forged owner key would run into.

Approve the payment on the Ledger

The payment fields go to the Quantum Administrator's Ledger below. Page through every screen, then Approve: the device signs the exact payment with ECDSA and with XMSS over one EIP-712 digest and consumes one one-time leaf. The pre-approval is then stored in the Guard.

Quantum Administrator's Ledger · FermionWallet XMSS app simulated

Connecting to device…

The FermionWallet Ledger app isn't built yet, so this device is simulated. It follows the app spec: the device holds the keys, picks the leaf from its own counter, shows every signed field, and signs only after you page through every screen and press Approve. Rejecting uses no leaf.

Pay again, now with the pre-approval

Propose the same payout, or execute the one from step 1 if it's still queued. It's the same owner-signed Safe transaction. This time the Guard finds the matching pre-approval, marks it used and lets the payment through.
