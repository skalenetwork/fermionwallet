Quantum protection

FermionWallet adds a second, post-quantum authorization layer to this Safe: every transaction must carry a hybrid ECDSA + XMSS (RFC 8391) pre-approval signed on a Ledger, enforced on-chain by the FermionWallet Guard. Your owner keys and address stay exactly as they are — the Guard is an add-on, and it is removable by design (no-brick escape hatch).

Onboarding preflight: before enrollment, the Safe's fallback handler must be removed (ERC-1271 isValidSignature would otherwise bypass the Guard), the Safe must have no enabled modules (modules skip the transaction Guard) unless it is on Safe 1.5+ with the FermionWallet module guard wired too, and existing token / Permit2 allowances must be revoked. This app checks the handler and modules above; allowances are not checked here.

The Guard contracts are in pre-audit early access and are not yet deployed to production networks. Enrollment from this app is enabled once canonical deployments are published in deployments.json.
