The threat

Your multisig is not a quantum hedge

Every Safe owner signs with ECDSA. A large enough quantum computer breaks ECDSA — for all of your signers at once. Adding more signers doesn't add a second cryptographic domain.

Multisig is governance

It decides who may sign. It says nothing about what happens when the signature scheme itself falls.

Hardware is operational security

Keeping keys on devices stops theft. It doesn't stop a forged signature over the same curve.

Off-chain policy is advice

A policy engine the chain doesn't consult can be bypassed. The last word has to be on-chain.

How it works

Two cryptographic domains.
One execution path.

FermionWallet installs as a Safe Guard — Safe's official pre-execution hook. Your owners keep signing exactly as they do today. The Guard refuses to let the transaction run unless it also carries a matching post-quantum pre-approval.

First signature

Your Safe owners

Existing owners, existing threshold, existing hardware wallets. Nothing to migrate. Protects against rogue operators, lost devices and internal failures.

Second signature

A quantum-resistant key

A Quantum Administrator signs a time-bound, single-use pre-approval with an XMSS key (RFC 8391, NIST SP 800-208) held on a Ledger. Protects against compromised signers, replay, allowance drains — and future quantum attacks.

Approvals bind to exact payloads, never to intents

A pre-approval names the Safe, chain, token, recipient, amount, validity window and policy. Change any byte of the queued transaction and the approval stops matching — the Guard reverts.

  • Time-bound: a validity window with a 15-minute minimum.
  • Single-use: consumed atomically when the transaction executes.
  • Domain-separated: EIP-712 over Safe, chain and policy hash.
  • Loud, not silent: denials are signed and logged just like approvals.

The Safe App overlays quantum status on your existing queue

Lives inside Safe{Wallet}

FermionWallet ships as a Safe App, so treasury teams stay in the interface they already use. Every pending transaction gets a traffic light and automatic risk flags — first-time recipient, unusual amount, incomplete owner signatures, or a selector the Guard will reject anyway.

The queue is ordered by Safe nonce, so approvals go top-down and never expire waiting behind an earlier transaction.

The device is the final authority — no blind signing

What you confirm on the device is what the chain enforces

The FermionWallet Ledger app pages through token, amount, full recipient address, validity window in UTC, Safe and nonce, and the policy fingerprint. The leaf counter is shown on every signature — an unexpected jump means someone tried to burn signatures.

Rejecting is always the easiest physical action, and costs nothing.

Leaf reuse is impossible by construction

XMSS is stateful: signing twice with one leaf would let an attacker forge. The Ledger commits its leaf counter before releasing a signature, and the contract keeps a used-leaf bitmap that reverts on any reuse.

function verifyAndConsume(
    bytes32 messageDigest,
    XMSS.Signature memory sig
) external {
    uint32 leaf = sig.leafIdx;
    if (isLeafUsed(leaf)) revert LeafAlreadyUsed(leaf);

    _markUsed(leaf);                 // burn the one-time leaf

    if (!XMSS.verify(messageDigest, sig, pk))
        revert InvalidSignature();  // whole tx rolls back

    emit LeafConsumed(leaf, messageDigest);
}
Security posture

Hard to get around.
Impossible to brick.

The Guard is the last on-chain veto — and it's designed so a lost key or a bug can never freeze your funds.

Fail-closed

Non-upgradeable Guard. A fast deny-all pause for incidents. No admin backdoor.

No module bypass

Enabled modules skip transaction guards, so enrollment requires no modules — or a paired module guard on Safe 1.5+.

48-hour admin timelock

Removing the Guard or changing owners needs a quantum approval and a public countdown. Any single owner can revoke it with one click.

14-day emergency exit

Ledger lost? Owners alone can remove the Guard after a long, loud timelock. A dead device never freezes the Safe.

No allowance drains

approve, permit and transferFrom are rejected outright. Batches are re-decoded leg by leg on-chain.

Co-signed key ceremony

The quantum key is generated inside the Ledger and registered in one transaction co-signed by owners over the full key root.

Governance changes are loud and slow; revocation is quiet and fast

Even a stolen quantum key can't quietly take over

Every administrative pre-approval emits an on-chain event and notifies every owner the moment it's created. It cannot execute until the timelock ends, and any owner can cancel it during the wait.
